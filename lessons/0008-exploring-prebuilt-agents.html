Lesson 8: Exploring Pre-built Agents

Duration: 12 minutes | Level: Beginner | Prerequisites: Lesson 7

Learning Objective

Discover the pre-built AI agents ServiceNow ships out of the box, and learn why exploring an existing agent is the fastest way to learn before building your own.

Why Start With Pre-built Agents?

ServiceNow ships a growing library of enterprise AI agents you can use and customize. Opening one and studying how it's wired — its instructions, tools, and structure — is the single best way to learn the patterns before you build from scratch.

Which pre-built agents you see depends on your release, licensed products, and enabled plugins. Here are verified examples by product area:

ITSM (IT Service Management)

Shipped as agentic workflows that chain specialist agents:

  • Triage and Categorize ITSM Incidents — sets category/subcategory and identifies the affected CI
  • Investigate and Resolve ITSM Incidents
  • Wrap Up and Resolve Incidents
  • Generate My Work Plan

(ServiceNow — Agentic Workflows End-to-End Setup Guide)

CSM (Customer Service Management)

  • Triage Cases (agentic workflow)
  • Troubleshooting Steps Identification (agent)
  • Customer 360 Insights
  • AI Voice Agents for CSM

(ServiceNow — Otto for CSM)

HR (HRSD)

  • Agentic capabilities such as recruiter draft-message review, and employee help agents

(ServiceNow — Agentic AI for HR)

How to explore one: In AI Agent Studio, open the agent/workflow library, pick one relevant to your licensed products, and inspect its identity fields, its tools, and how (if it's a workflow) its agents are sequenced. Notice how they apply everything from Units 4–6.
⚠ Verify against your release. The exact catalog of out-of-box agents changes by release and license. Always confirm what's available by checking your instance's release notes and the AI Agent Studio library — don't assume a specific agent exists until you see it.
Learning tactic: Before Unit 4's hands-on build, open one pre-built agent and answer: What is its scope (description)? Its identity (agentRole)? What tools does it have, and are they supervised or autonomous? You'll recognize every one of these concepts now.
