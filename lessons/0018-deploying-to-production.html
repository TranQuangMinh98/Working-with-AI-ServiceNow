Lesson 19: Deploying to Production & Governance

Duration: 15 minutes | Level: Intermediate | Prerequisites: Lesson 18

Learning Objective

Take an agent live safely: choose the right channel, activate its trigger, and govern it with the AI Control Tower and built-in guardrails.

Step 1: Choose a Channel

A channel is where users reach your deployed agent. Pick based on where your users already are:

  • Otto Panel (Now Assist Panel) — the in-product side panel
  • Virtual Agent — conversational chat (no trigger required)
  • Analysis Panel — inline on a record form
  • Microsoft Teams, Slack, SMS, email, web chat — external surfaces

Step 2: Activate the Trigger

⚠ The #1 deployment gotcha: Triggers deploy inactive. Your agent will do nothing on its own until you manually activate the trigger — and you should only do that after testing confirms correct behavior. Then monitor the first real runs closely.

Step 3: Govern with the AI Control Tower

The AI Control Tower (expanded in the Zurich release) is the central command center to discover, govern, secure, observe, and measure every AI agent, model, and workflow on your instance. AI assets are tracked in the CMDB.

Golden rule of production: Always deploy agents under the Control Tower — never as unmanaged, invisible automation. If you can't see it, you can't govern it. (ServiceNow — AI Control Tower)

The Guardrails You Get

ServiceNow ships several safety mechanisms — know their names:

Deny-by-default ACL Agents can only access what you explicitly permit.
Role masking Limits which roles/data the agent effectively sees.
Kill Switch Instantly stop an agent that misbehaves.
Now Assist Guardian Monitors and guards generative behavior.
Supervised mode Human approves risky tool calls (from Lesson 12).
A safe go-live checklist:
  1. Test manually + test user access (multiple runs)
  2. Set write-tools to Supervised; reads to Autonomous
  3. Confirm ACLs are least-privilege
  4. Select the channel; keep audience small at first
  5. Activate the trigger
  6. Watch first runs in the Control Tower; widen access gradually

Note: ServiceNow's governance is built on role/ACL controls, manual trigger activation, and Control Tower monitoring rather than a single mandatory sign-off step. Approval gates are implemented via supervised execution and instructions.
